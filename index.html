About the state protocol

RS uses the zero joint pose. AS geometry averages five synchronized states: t = 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1. Revolute joints follow a signed 90° span; prismatic joints follow their evaluation travel. AOR uses the full evaluation range and ten states in formal evaluation; its slider here is a continuous pose preview.

Appearance uses the published five-state, eight-view reference images. A valid score requires a matching prediction and the evaluator’s recorded protocol. This illustration does not compute benchmark scores.
